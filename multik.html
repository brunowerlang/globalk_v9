About Multi-K

Multi-K is a brand under the Global-K Group, specializing in smart organizational solutions. We offer innovative products designed to simplify storage and everyday life — combining functionality, quality, and style.

Our product line includes cable organizers, cable tags, practical packaging, and space-saving solutions for both home and office. Every item is thoughtfully designed for consumers who value convenience without compromising on aesthetics.
